Pyramid Workshop asset notes

The history collection uses real present-day monument photographs. Photographer credits, source links, image licenses and resizing notices are displayed beside each photograph and included in history.json.

Distant landscape illustrations and the detailed broadleaf texture are AI-generated scenic art, not historical photographs or archaeological reconstructions. The Sphinx model is an original interpretive sculpture shown only in a labeled later-Giza view. The quarry limestone surface (quarry-limestone-v2.jpg) and date-palm frond cutout (date-palm-frond-v2.png) were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this project. They are illustrative game textures, not archaeological photographs. Other material textures and construction props are original procedural game assets.

Dimensions, material prices, boat capacity, construction pieces and flood levels are teaching simplifications. Sourced historical stories and reconstructed worksite explanations are available within the game.
